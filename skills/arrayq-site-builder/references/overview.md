# Site Builder Overview

How ArrayQ Site Builder works. Build a static marketing website, publish it on ArrayQ and start taking bookings through the built-in checkout, sign in and customer account pages.

Source: [https://arrayq.com/docs/site-builder](https://arrayq.com/docs/site-builder), last updated 2026-11-07.

Site Builder lets you host your own website on ArrayQ and connect it to your account. Visitors see your services, pick one and book it. Each booking shows up in your dashboard as a new job, ready for your team.

You bring the marketing pages: a home page, and a blog or location pages if you want them. It can be a template, a site you designed yourself or one generated by an AI tool with the [Build with AI](build-with-ai.md) prompt. ArrayQ hosts it and adds the rest: a checkout, customer sign in, and a customer account with order tracking, invoices and live chat. See [Built-in pages](built-in-pages.md).

## How it fits together

| Piece | What it does | Where you manage it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your website | Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript marketing pages | Your computer or code editor |
| Built-in pages | Checkout, sign in, registration and the customer account at `/checkout/SERVICE_ID`, `/login`, `/register` and `/app` | Included, nothing to build |
| `config.js` | Gives your scripts the API address and your site key | Served by ArrayQ on your hosted site |
| Services API | Lists your services on your pages | Called from your website's JavaScript |
| Hosting | Serves your files on an ArrayQ link | Site Builder, **Builder** tab |
| Tracker | Records page views, clicks and form submissions | Site Builder, **Tracker** tab |
| Orca chatbot | Answers visitor questions on your site | Site Builder, **Orca chatbot** tab |
| Custom domain | Serves the site on your own domain | Site Builder, **Domain** tab |

## The end-to-end flow

1. **Build your site**

   Create a folder with an `index.html` at the top, or let an AI tool build it with the [Build with AI](build-with-ai.md) prompt. See [Build your site](build-your-site.md).

2. **Connect your site**

   Load `./config.js` before your script and read `window.SITE_CONFIG`. ArrayQ serves the file, so there is no key to add. See [Connect your site](connect-your-site.md).

3. **Show your services**

   Add a small script that loads your services and links each one to `/checkout/SERVICE_ID`. See [Services and booking](services-and-booking.md#list-services).

4. **Link to the built-in pages**

   Point your **Sign in** and **My orders** buttons to `/login` and `/app`, and your **Book now** buttons to your service list. See [Built-in pages](built-in-pages.md).

5. **Publish**

   Upload the folder in the **Builder** tab and click **Publish site**. See [Publish your site](publish.md).

6. **Turn on the extras**

   Switch on the [Tracker](tracker.md) and the [Orca chatbot](orca-chatbot.md), then connect a [custom domain](custom-domain.md).

## Example project

A typical site is a plain static folder with no framework. There is no checkout or account page in it, because ArrayQ provides those:

```text
my-site/
  index.html          Home page with the service list
  about.html          Optional extra pages
  locations/
    austin.html       Optional location pages
  app.js              Loads services and handles the menu
  styles.css          Styling
  assets/
    favicon.svg
```

> **Note**
>
> You can start even smaller. A single `index.html` with a script tag is enough to list services and send visitors to the built-in checkout.

## Who can use Site Builder

Business owners and admins can open Site Builder. Crew members need the **Site builder** permission. Your plan must include Site Builder, or your published site shows a "Site unavailable" page.
