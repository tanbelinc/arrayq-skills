# Industries

The trades ArrayQ is built for, each with its own page on the ArrayQ website.

- [Junk Removal Business Software](https://arrayq.com/industries/junk-removal): Price hauling jobs by item, schedule pickups and manage customer records with ArrayQ. Invoice the work, depending on your plan, and get WeCycle job requests.
- [Cleaning Business Software](https://arrayq.com/industries/cleaning): Manage cleaning schedules, client notes and crew access with ArrayQ, and invoice clients when your plan includes it. Built for home and office cleaning teams.
- [Pest Control Business Software](https://arrayq.com/industries/pest-control): Keep pest control visit notes, photos and schedules together in ArrayQ. Coordinate technicians and, depending on your plan, text customers and send invoices.
- [Plumbing Business Software](https://arrayq.com/industries/plumbing): Manage plumbing enquiries and schedules with ArrayQ, and give plumbers mobile access to customer details, job notes and photos. Invoicing depends on your plan.
- [Construction Business Software](https://arrayq.com/industries/construction): Keep construction clients, crews, quotes and site notes organized with ArrayQ. Chat with your crew, and invoice clients when your plan includes it.
- [Handyman Business Software](https://arrayq.com/industries/handyman): Plan handyman visits, price several tasks per job and manage customer details with ArrayQ. Invoice the work and take card payments when your plan includes it.
- [Electrical Business Software](https://arrayq.com/industries/electrical): Keep electrical job photos, notes and schedules together with ArrayQ. Coordinate electricians, control team access and invoice when your plan includes it.
- [Lawn Care Business Software](https://arrayq.com/industries/lawn-care): Manage lawn care visits, yard notes and seasonal crews with ArrayQ. Turn completed jobs into invoices and track payments when your plan includes invoicing.
- [Pool Service Business Software](https://arrayq.com/industries/pool-service): ArrayQ helps pool service companies keep pool and customer notes, document each visit with photos, schedule technicians and send invoices.
- [Tree Care Business Software](https://arrayq.com/industries/tree-care): ArrayQ helps tree care and arborist businesses quote on the job, document sites with photos, coordinate crews and track what customers owe.
- [Landscaping Business Software](https://arrayq.com/industries/landscaping): ArrayQ helps landscaping businesses capture leads from their website, organize crews, keep project notes and invoice installs and upkeep.
- [Painting Business Software](https://arrayq.com/industries/painting): ArrayQ helps painting contractors quote on the job, keep color choices and photos on each job, schedule crews and track what clients owe.
