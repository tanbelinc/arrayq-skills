# Industries

The trades ArrayQ is built for, each with its own page on the ArrayQ website.

- [Junk Removal Business Software](https://arrayq.com/industries/junk-removal): ArrayQ helps junk removal and hauling companies price jobs by item, schedule pickups, invoice customers and receive job requests from WeCycle.
- [Cleaning Business Software](https://arrayq.com/industries/cleaning): ArrayQ helps residential and commercial cleaning companies keep client notes, schedule cleans on one calendar, manage cleaner access and invoice.
- [Pest Control Business Software](https://arrayq.com/industries/pest-control): ArrayQ helps pest control companies keep property notes and treatment photos on each job, schedule technicians, text customers and send invoices.
- [Plumbing Business Software](https://arrayq.com/industries/plumbing): ArrayQ helps plumbing businesses log every call as a lead, turn it into a job with photos and notes, and invoice with an online payment link.
- [Construction Business Software](https://arrayq.com/industries/construction): ArrayQ helps construction contractors manage client records, crew access, team chat, estimates and invoices from one dashboard.
- [Handyman Business Software](https://arrayq.com/industries/handyman): ArrayQ helps handyman businesses price varied tasks, keep a full calendar organized and get paid by card, cash or check after each visit.
- [Electrical Business Software](https://arrayq.com/industries/electrical): ArrayQ helps electrical contractors keep job notes and photos, give electricians the right access, message the team and invoice clients.
- [Lawn Care Business Software](https://arrayq.com/industries/lawn-care): ArrayQ helps lawn care businesses keep customer notes, see every booked visit on the calendar, add seasonal crew and get paid online.
- [Pool Service Business Software](https://arrayq.com/industries/pool-service): ArrayQ helps pool service companies keep pool and customer notes, document each visit with photos, schedule technicians and send invoices.
- [Tree Care Business Software](https://arrayq.com/industries/tree-care): ArrayQ helps tree care and arborist businesses send estimates, document sites with photos, coordinate crews and track partial payments.
- [Landscaping Business Software](https://arrayq.com/industries/landscaping): ArrayQ helps landscaping businesses capture leads from their website, organize crews, keep project notes and invoice installs and upkeep.
- [Painting Business Software](https://arrayq.com/industries/painting): ArrayQ helps painting contractors send estimates, keep color choices and photos on each job, schedule crews and track what clients owe.
