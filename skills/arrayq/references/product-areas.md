# Product areas

What each part of ArrayQ does, taken from the feature pages of the ArrayQ website.

## Lead Management

Capture every enquiry and turn more of them into customers. Page: [https://arrayq.com/features/leads](https://arrayq.com/features/leads).

Every enquiry goes into one list. You see where it came from, its status and what comes next. When the customer says yes, you turn it into a client in a few clicks.

- Every enquiry in one list
- The source of each lead, from your website, referrals, ads, calls and texts
- Status and source filters, so the leads that need a reply are easy to find
- One step to turn a lead into a client, with your notes carried over

**What is lead management software?**
It keeps every enquiry in one place. You see who asked, what they need and what has happened since, so you reply on time and win more jobs.

**What lead sources can ArrayQ track?**
You can mark leads as coming from your website, a referral, social media, Google Ads, direct mail or a cold call. Leads from the Orca AI chatbot, and from calls, missed calls and texts to your business number, are tagged with their source for you. Those two depend on your plan.

**Can I turn a lead into a client?**
Yes. Converting a lead creates a client record with the lead's contact details, and the lead's notes stay visible on the client profile. Nothing has to be entered twice.

**What separates a lead from a client?**
A lead is someone who has asked about your service. A client is someone who has agreed to hire you. ArrayQ moves a person from one to the other without losing any details.

## Client Management

Every customer's details and notes in one profile. Page: [https://arrayq.com/features/clients](https://arrayq.com/features/clients).

Every customer's contact details and notes in one profile. Your office and crew members with permission can work from the same record.

- One profile per customer, with contact details and address
- Client type, source and status on every record
- Notes carried over from the original lead
- Search and filters to find any client fast

**What is client management software?**
It is a tool that keeps your customers' details and history in one place. You can see who they are, how to reach them, where they came from and what has been said, so you stop relying on memory and scattered notes.

**What information does a client profile hold?**
Name, email, phone and address. It also holds the client type, lead source, status, the date they became a client, notes from the original lead and your own notes.

**Can my crew see client details on their phones?**
Yes. ArrayQ works in any browser and in the ArrayQ apps for iPhone and Android. Each crew member sees only the areas you have given them permission to use.

**What is the difference between a lead and a client?**
A lead is someone who has asked about your service. A client is someone who has agreed to hire you. When you convert a lead, ArrayQ moves their details and notes across so nothing is lost.

## Job Management

Create, schedule and track every job from booking to payment. Page: [https://arrayq.com/features/jobs](https://arrayq.com/features/jobs).

Create the job, schedule it, send your crew and get paid, all from one record. No more piecing a job together from texts and sticky notes.

- Step-by-step job creation for a new or existing client
- Your services, items and prices on every job, plus custom pricing when you need it
- Photos, special instructions and internal notes
- Status tracking from pending to completed

**What is job management software?**
It's a tool that keeps everything about a job in one place: who it's for, what needs doing, when, how much it costs and whether it's been paid. It helps you stay organized and get through the day with fewer phone calls.

**Can I add photos to a job?**
Yes. Upload photos to a job so your crew can see what to expect, and you have a record of the work.

**Can I set my own prices for services?**
Yes. Create your services, categories, and specific items with your own prices. You can change the price on any individual job when you need to.

**How do I record payment when a job is finished?**
Complete the job. If its invoice is still unpaid, ArrayQ asks how it was paid, so you can mark it paid by cash, check or Zelle or schedule an email reminder with a card payment link. Payments sit on the job's invoice, so this needs a plan that includes invoicing.

## Invoicing

Send clear invoices and follow up on what you are owed. Page: [https://arrayq.com/features/invoicing](https://arrayq.com/features/invoicing).

Create the invoice from the job, email it with a secure online payment link and keep every unpaid balance in view.

- Invoices generated directly from your jobs
- Email delivery with a secure online payment link
- A status on every invoice, from unpaid to paid
- Scheduled email reminders for unpaid invoices

**What is invoicing software?**
Invoicing software creates invoices, sends them to customers and records which ones are paid. You spend less time on paperwork and always know what you are owed.

**Can customers pay an invoice online?**
Yes, when your plan includes invoicing. Invoices are emailed with a secure link, and the customer pays by card through Stripe. You connect your own Stripe account to receive payments.

**Does ArrayQ support estimates?**
Not as a separate document. Put the quoted services and prices on the job while it is pending. When the customer agrees and your plan includes invoicing, create the invoice from the same figures.

**Can ArrayQ remind customers about unpaid invoices?**
Yes, when your plan includes invoicing. When you complete a job that still has an unpaid invoice, you can schedule an email reminder with a payment link.

## Payments

Take card payments online and keep every payment on record. Page: [https://arrayq.com/features/payments](https://arrayq.com/features/payments).

When your plan includes invoicing, customers pay by card straight from their invoice, and you mark cash, check or Zelle payments as you complete the job. Every payment shows in one place.

- Card payments through your own Stripe account
- A payment link sent with every invoice
- Cash, check and Zelle marked on the job's invoice
- Every payment tied to its job and invoice

**Which payment methods can I accept?**
Card payments online through Stripe, when your plan includes invoicing. If a customer pays by cash, check, Zelle or another method, you mark the invoice paid when you complete the job.

**Do I need my own Stripe account?**
Yes. You connect your own Stripe account from the Payments page, so card payments go to your business.

**Can I track partial payments and refund statuses?**
Refunds, yes. Partial payments, no. ArrayQ records an invoice as paid in full, by card or by cash, check or Zelle. If a card payment is refunded in full or in part, the invoice shows it as refunded or as a partial refund.

**When should I record a cash, check or Zelle payment?**
When you complete the job. If the job's invoice is still unpaid, ArrayQ asks how it was paid, and the invoice is marked paid with that method. This works on plans that include invoicing.

## Scheduling & Calendar

See every booked job by day, week or month, or as a list. Page: [https://arrayq.com/features/calendar](https://arrayq.com/features/calendar).

See every booked job by day, week or month, or as a list, and filter by status to know what's coming up and what needs your attention.

- Day, week, month and list views
- Jobs placed on the calendar from their schedule
- A filter by job status
- Works in the browser and in the iPhone and Android apps

**What calendar views does ArrayQ have?**
Day, week, month and list views, with a filter for job status.

**Can I reschedule jobs in ArrayQ?**
Yes. Change the job's date and time, and the calendar moves it for you.

**Can I see all my upcoming jobs in ArrayQ?**
Yes. ArrayQ's calendar shows your scheduled jobs in one place. You can switch between day, week, month and list views to plan your work.

**Can I check completed jobs in ArrayQ?**
Yes. Use the job status filter to show completed jobs, or narrow the calendar to work that is still pending.

**Can I manage multiple job appointments in ArrayQ?**
Yes. Every scheduled job shows on the same calendar, so you can see the whole day's workload at once.

**Does ArrayQ help me avoid scheduling conflicts?**
ArrayQ doesn't block overlapping bookings for you. It shows every scheduled job on one calendar, so you can check what is already booked before you add a new job.

**Can I access my ArrayQ calendar while working outside the office?**
Yes. Open ArrayQ in a mobile browser or in the iPhone and Android apps to check your schedule on the go.

## Crew Management

Bring your team in and control what each person can see and do. Page: [https://arrayq.com/features/crew-management](https://arrayq.com/features/crew-management).

Bring your team into ArrayQ and decide exactly what each person can see and do. Everyone gets the access their job needs, and nothing more.

- Invite crew members to your workspace
- Organize people into crew groups
- Permissions set per person, for each area of ArrayQ
- View, create, edit, and delete rights set separately

**Can I limit what a crew member can see?**
Yes. For each crew member, you can choose which areas they can access and whether they can view, create, edit or delete information.

**Do crew members need their own login?**
Yes. Each invited person uses their own account, with permissions set for their role.

**Can I update crew member permissions in ArrayQ?**
Yes. You can change permissions whenever someone's role or responsibilities change.

**Can I remove a crew member from ArrayQ?**
Yes. You can remove access when someone leaves your team or no longer needs an account.

**How do I invite a new crew member to ArrayQ?**
Invite the person to your ArrayQ workspace, then set their permissions based on their responsibilities.

## Team Chat

Keep team conversations inside ArrayQ instead of personal texts. Page: [https://arrayq.com/features/team-chat](https://arrayq.com/features/team-chat).

Message your crew in real time, share photos and files, and keep every work conversation in the same app you use to run the business.

- Direct and group conversations
- Real-time messages with typing indicators
- Share files and photos
- Works on desktop, iPhone, and Android

**Can I send files and photos in ArrayQ Team Chat?**
Yes. You can attach files and photos to messages to share important documents, job details or images with your team.

**Does ArrayQ Team Chat work on phones?**
Yes. You can access Team Chat through your browser or the ArrayQ apps for iPhone and Android. This makes it easier to stay connected while working in the field.

**Can I message my whole crew at once?**
Yes. You can create group conversations for your entire crew, a specific truck or your office team. This helps everyone stay updated without sending separate messages.

**Can I message one person privately?**
Yes. ArrayQ supports direct conversations alongside group chats. You can send a private message when you need to discuss something with a specific team member.

**Can I use group chats for different teams?**
Yes. You can create separate conversations for different crews, trucks or office teams. This keeps discussions organized and relevant to the right people.

## Customer Text Messaging

Text customers from your business inbox, not your personal phone. Page: [https://arrayq.com/features/text-messaging](https://arrayq.com/features/text-messaging).

Text customers from your ArrayQ business number and a shared inbox, not your personal phone. New numbers become leads, and anyone who replies STOP is opted out for you.

- Two-way texts with customers in one inbox
- Conversations your office team can share
- Texts from new numbers recorded as leads with their source
- Automatic opt-out when a customer replies STOP

**Can customers text my business in ArrayQ?**
Yes, once you have an ArrayQ business number. Customers text that number, and you send and receive messages from your ArrayQ inbox without switching apps.

**What happens when someone texts from a new number?**
ArrayQ can record the message as a lead and track its source. You can then follow up and turn the enquiry into a new client.

**Can more than one person reply to customer texts?**
Yes. Every team member with inbox access can read and reply to customer messages, so a conversation doesn't stall when one person is out.

**Do I have to use my personal phone?**
No. Customer texts go through your ArrayQ business number and inbox, so your personal number stays private.

**What happens when a customer replies STOP?**
When a customer replies STOP, ArrayQ records their request and stops sending text messages to that number.

**What do I need to text customers?**
A business number from ArrayQ, which depends on your plan, and a completed texting registration. Customers text that number, and you reply from your ArrayQ inbox. Texts use ArrayQ credits, and ArrayQ shows any one-time setup fee before you claim the number.

## Orca AI Chatbot

Answer website visitors instantly and capture them as leads. Page: [https://arrayq.com/features/ai-chatbot](https://arrayq.com/features/ai-chatbot).

Answer website visitors instantly, even after hours. Orca replies from a knowledge base you control and turns interested visitors into leads in ArrayQ.

- A chat widget for your website
- Answers from a knowledge base you write
- A lead form that creates leads in ArrayQ
- Conversation history and analytics

**Where does Orca get its answers?**
From the knowledge base you set up in ArrayQ, such as your services, service areas, hours and policies. Orca is only as good as the information you give it, so start with the questions customers ask you most often.

**What should I put in my knowledge base?**
Begin with the basics: the services you offer, the areas you cover, your opening hours and your policies. You can add more over time as you see what visitors ask.

**How do chatbot leads become booked jobs?**
Orca captures the visitor's contact details as a lead in ArrayQ, linked to the conversation. Your team reviews the enquiry, follows up and creates a job when the customer is ready.

**What happens when a visitor shares their details?**
Orca creates a lead in ArrayQ with the chatbot as its source. The saved conversation links to the lead, so you can see what they asked.

**Does Orca work with my current website?**
Yes, when your plan includes the Orca AI chatbot. Add the Orca widget to your existing website, or enable it on your ArrayQ-hosted site.

**Will Orca answer questions when I'm closed?**
Yes. Visitors can ask at any time, and Orca replies from your knowledge base.

## Website Hosting

Host your business website on your own domain with ArrayQ. Page: [https://arrayq.com/features/website](https://arrayq.com/features/website).

Publish your business website on your own domain, from the same account where you manage leads and jobs. No separate hosting bill to keep track of.

- Upload and publish your website files
- Connect a custom domain you already own
- Guided DNS verification
- Visitor tracking and the Orca AI chatbot on your pages

**Does ArrayQ design my website?**
No. ArrayQ is not a drag-and-drop page designer. It hosts the theme files you upload. You or your designer can build them, or paste the prompt from Build Your Site with AI into an AI tool to get a site ready to upload. Build Your Site explains how the files fit together.

**What do I need to get started?**
Your theme files and a plan that includes website hosting. Once published, your site works on its ArrayQ link. To use your own domain, you also need access to the place where you manage its DNS settings.

**Can I use my own domain?**
Yes. Connect a custom domain, add the DNS record ArrayQ shows you, and ArrayQ checks it and sets up HTTPS before the site goes live on it.

**Is connecting a domain difficult?**
Not usually. You add one DNS record, and ArrayQ checks it for you. If you have a web designer, they can add it for you.

**Do I need separate hosting?**
No. When your plan includes website hosting, ArrayQ hosts your site, so you don't need a separate hosting provider.

**Can I add a chatbot to my site?**
Yes, when your plan includes the Orca AI chatbot. Turn it on and Orca answers questions from your knowledge base and captures leads in ArrayQ.

## WeCycle Job Requests

Get junk removal jobs from WeCycle in your ArrayQ dashboard. Page: [https://arrayq.com/features/wecycle-jobs](https://arrayq.com/features/wecycle-jobs).

Connect ArrayQ to WeCycle, the junk removal booking platform, and receive job requests in the same dashboard where you run the rest of your work.

- Connect your ArrayQ account to WeCycle
- Receive job requests from WeCycle customers
- Request the jobs that fit your schedule
- Manage WeCycle work alongside your other jobs

**What is WeCycle?**
WeCycle is a junk removal booking platform. It books customers online, and once you connect, jobs from WeCycle can be offered to your business inside ArrayQ.

**Who can receive WeCycle job requests?**
Junk removal and hauling businesses that use ArrayQ and connect the WeCycle integration can receive job requests offered to their business.

**How do I connect to WeCycle?**
Open Integrations in your ArrayQ dashboard, choose WeCycle and follow the steps to connect your account.

**Do I have to take every job?**
No. You request the jobs that fit your schedule.

**Where do WeCycle jobs appear?**
In the same dashboard as your own jobs, so you can see your whole workload in one place.

**Does my team need another app?**
No. Accepted WeCycle jobs are handled in your ArrayQ dashboard.

**Does WeCycle charge fees or commission?**
Contact the team to confirm current WeCycle fees or commissions before requesting work.

**How many jobs will I get?**
Connecting gives you access to job requests that may be offered to your business. It does not guarantee a fixed number of jobs.
