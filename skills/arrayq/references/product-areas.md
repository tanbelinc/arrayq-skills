# Product areas

What each part of ArrayQ does, taken from the feature pages of the ArrayQ website.

## Lead Management

Capture every enquiry and turn more of them into customers. Page: [https://arrayq.com/features/leads](https://arrayq.com/features/leads).

Keep every enquiry in one list, see where it came from, move it through your statuses and convert it into a client when the customer says yes.

- Every enquiry in one list or card view
- Lead sources from website, referrals, ads, calls and texts
- Status tabs and filters so follow-ups are easy to find
- Convert a lead into a client with its notes carried over

**What lead sources can ArrayQ track?**
You can mark leads as coming from your website, a referral, social media, Google Ads, direct mail or a cold call. Leads captured by the ArrayQ AI chatbot, phone calls, missed calls and inbound text messages are tagged with their source automatically.

**Can I turn a lead into a client?**
Yes. Converting a lead creates a client record with the lead's contact details and notes, so nothing has to be entered twice.

## Client Management

Every customer's details and notes in one profile. Page: [https://arrayq.com/features/clients](https://arrayq.com/features/clients).

Keep your customer list organized. Each client profile holds contact details, address, client type, source, status and notes that your whole team can see.

- One profile per customer with contact details and address
- Client type, source and status on every record
- Notes carried over from the original lead
- Search, filter and switch between list and card views

**What information does a client profile hold?**
Name, email, phone, address, client type, lead source, status, the date they became a client, notes from the original lead and any additional notes you add.

**Can my crew see client details on their phones?**
Yes. ArrayQ runs in any browser and in the ArrayQ apps for iPhone and Android, and each crew member sees the areas you give them permission to use.

## Job Management

Create, schedule and track every job from booking to payment. Page: [https://arrayq.com/features/jobs](https://arrayq.com/features/jobs).

Create jobs for your clients with the services or items involved, the schedule and price, attach photos and notes, and move each job through its status until it is completed and paid.

- Step-by-step job creation for a new or existing client
- Services, specific items and custom prices on each job
- Photos, special instructions and internal notes
- Status tracking from booked to completed

**Can I add photos to a job?**
Yes. Upload photos to a job so the crew can see what to expect and you have a record of the work.

**How do I record payment when a job is finished?**
Complete the job and record the payment as cash, check or Zelle, or send the invoice so the customer can pay online by card through Stripe.

**Can I set my own prices for services?**
Yes. Create your services, categories and specific items with your prices, and override the price on any job when you need to.

## Invoicing

Send clear invoices and follow up on what you are owed. Page: [https://arrayq.com/features/invoicing](https://arrayq.com/features/invoicing).

Create invoices for your jobs, email them to customers with a secure link to pay online, see each invoice's status at a glance and schedule reminders for unpaid balances.

- Invoices created from your jobs
- Email delivery with a secure online payment link
- Statuses including estimate, pending, partially paid and paid
- Scheduled email reminders for unpaid invoices

**Can customers pay an invoice online?**
Yes. Invoices are emailed with a secure link where the customer can pay by card through Stripe. You connect your own Stripe account to receive the money.

**Does ArrayQ support estimates?**
Yes. An invoice can be saved with the estimate status before the job is confirmed.

**Can ArrayQ remind customers about unpaid invoices?**
Yes. When you complete a job that still has an unpaid invoice, you can schedule an email reminder to the customer.

## Payments

Take card payments online and keep every payment on record. Page: [https://arrayq.com/features/payments](https://arrayq.com/features/payments).

Connect your Stripe account to accept card payments from invoice links, record cash, check and Zelle payments against jobs, and see every payment in one place.

- Card payments through your own Stripe account
- Payment links sent with your invoices
- Record cash, check and Zelle payments
- Every payment tied to its job and invoice

**Which payment methods can I accept?**
Card payments online through Stripe, plus cash, check and Zelle payments that you record in ArrayQ.

**Do I need my own Stripe account?**
Yes. You connect your own Stripe account from the Payments page, so card payments go to your business.

**Can ArrayQ handle partial payments and refunds?**
Invoices track partially paid, refunded and partially refunded statuses, so you can see exactly where each balance stands.

## Scheduling & Calendar

See every booked job by day, week, month or year. Page: [https://arrayq.com/features/calendar](https://arrayq.com/features/calendar).

View your jobs on a calendar with day, week, month, year and agenda views, and filter by job status to see exactly what is booked and what needs attention.

- Day, week, month, year and agenda views
- Jobs placed on the calendar from their schedule
- Filter the calendar by job status
- Works in the browser and the iPhone and Android apps

**What calendar views does ArrayQ have?**
Day, week, month, year and agenda views, with a filter for job status.

**Do jobs appear on the calendar automatically?**
Yes. A job shows on the calendar from the date and time you schedule it for.

## Crew Management

Bring your team in and control what each person can see and do. Page: [https://arrayq.com/features/crew-management](https://arrayq.com/features/crew-management).

Invite crew members to your ArrayQ workspace, organize them into groups and choose exactly which areas each person can view, create, edit or delete in.

- Invite crew members to your workspace
- Organize people into crew groups
- Per-person permissions for each area of ArrayQ
- View, create, edit and delete rights set separately

**Can I limit what a crew member can see?**
Yes. For each crew member you choose which areas they can use and whether they can view, create, edit or delete in each one.

**Do crew members need their own login?**
Yes. Each person you invite gets their own account, so permissions and activity stay tied to the right person.

## Team Chat

Keep team conversations inside ArrayQ instead of personal texts. Page: [https://arrayq.com/features/team-chat](https://arrayq.com/features/team-chat).

Message your team in real time with direct and group conversations, typing indicators and file attachments, all inside the same app you use to run the business.

- Direct and group conversations
- Real-time messages with typing indicators
- Share files and photos
- Available on desktop, iPhone and Android

**Can I send files in team chat?**
Yes. You can attach files and photos to messages.

**Does team chat work on phones?**
Yes. Team chat works in the browser and in the ArrayQ apps for iPhone and Android.

## Customer Text Messaging

Text customers from your business inbox, not your personal phone. Page: [https://arrayq.com/features/text-messaging](https://arrayq.com/features/text-messaging).

Send and receive text messages with customers from a shared inbox in ArrayQ. Inbound texts can become leads, and customers who reply STOP are opted out automatically.

- Two-way SMS with customers in one inbox
- Conversations your office team can share
- Inbound texts recorded as leads with their source
- Opt-out handling when a customer replies STOP

**Can customers text my business in ArrayQ?**
Yes. Incoming and outgoing texts appear in the ArrayQ text messaging inbox, and a text from a new number can be recorded as a lead.

**What happens when a customer replies STOP?**
ArrayQ records the opt-out and stops sending messages to that number.

**Can more than one person reply to customer texts?**
Yes. The inbox is shared, so anyone on your team with access can read and answer conversations.

## Orca AI Chatbot

Answer website visitors instantly and capture them as leads. Page: [https://arrayq.com/features/ai-chatbot](https://arrayq.com/features/ai-chatbot).

Add Orca, ArrayQ's AI chatbot, to your website. It answers visitor questions from a knowledge base you control, collects contact details with a lead form and saves every conversation in ArrayQ.

- Chat widget for your website
- Answers from a knowledge base you write
- Lead form that creates leads in ArrayQ
- Conversation history and analytics

**Where does Orca get its answers?**
From the knowledge base you set up in ArrayQ, such as your services, service areas, hours and policies.

**Can Orca book jobs?**
Orca answers questions and captures leads with a contact form. You follow up and book the job in ArrayQ.

**Does Orca work with my current website?**
Yes. Add the Orca widget to your existing website, or host your site with ArrayQ and Orca is available there too.

## Website Hosting

Host your business website on your own domain with ArrayQ. Page: [https://arrayq.com/features/website](https://arrayq.com/features/website).

Upload your website files to ArrayQ and publish them on your own domain. ArrayQ checks your DNS setup, tracks visitors and can add the Orca AI chatbot to your pages.

- Upload and publish your website files
- Connect your own custom domain
- Guided DNS verification
- Visitor tracking and Orca AI chatbot on your pages

**Does ArrayQ design my website?**
ArrayQ hosts the website files you upload. It is not a drag-and-drop page designer, so bring a site you or your designer built.

**Can I use my own domain?**
Yes. Connect a custom domain and ArrayQ verifies your DNS settings before the site goes live on it.

## WeCycle Job Requests

Get junk removal jobs from WeCycle in your ArrayQ dashboard. Page: [https://arrayq.com/features/wecycle-jobs](https://arrayq.com/features/wecycle-jobs).

Connect ArrayQ to WeCycle, the junk removal booking platform, to receive job requests from WeCycle customers and handle the work alongside the rest of your jobs.

- Connect your ArrayQ account to WeCycle
- Receive job requests from WeCycle customers
- Request the jobs that fit your schedule
- Manage WeCycle work with your other jobs

**Who can receive WeCycle job requests?**
Junk removal and hauling businesses that use ArrayQ and connect the WeCycle integration.

**How do I connect to WeCycle?**
Open Integrations in your ArrayQ dashboard, choose WeCycle and follow the steps to connect your account.
