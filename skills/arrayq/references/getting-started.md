# ArrayQ Documentation

Guides for running your business on ArrayQ. Learn how to build a booking website, connect it to your account and manage every job from one place.

Source: [https://arrayq.com/docs](https://arrayq.com/docs), last updated 2026-10-20.

ArrayQ is an all-in-one CRM for service businesses. These docs explain how each part of ArrayQ works and how to connect your own tools to it.

## Where to start

- [Site Builder](https://arrayq.com/docs/site-builder): Build a website, publish it on ArrayQ and take bookings that land straight in your dashboard.
- [Services and booking API](https://arrayq.com/docs/site-builder/services-and-booking): Load your services and create bookings from your website. Hosted sites need no API key.
- [Build with AI](https://arrayq.com/docs/site-builder/build-with-ai): One copyable prompt that lets any AI tool build a ready-to-publish site for your business.
- [Built-in pages](https://arrayq.com/docs/site-builder/built-in-pages): Checkout, sign in and a customer account with order tracking and live chat, included with every site.
- [Custom domain](https://arrayq.com/docs/site-builder/custom-domain): Serve your published site on your own domain with HTTPS.

## What you need

- An ArrayQ account. Sign in at [app.arrayq.com](https://app.arrayq.com).
- A plan that includes **Site Builder** if you want to host your website on ArrayQ.
- At least one active service in your dashboard. Your website can only book services that are active.
